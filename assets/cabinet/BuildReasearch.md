### Cutting Wood at RPI
According to the [RPI Website](https://manufacturing.eng.rpi.edu/facilities/jecshop), there is a machine shop that is open to students Wed (5-8) and Fri(4-7).
In order to be allowed to work in the lab, students must:
- Complete the Lab Saftey Quiz
- Wear Saftey Goggles at all times
- Wear appropriate saftey equipment when using different machines

### Saftey Quiz

Saftey Quiz is not an in-person quiz, rather it is an online percipico course that can be taken anywhere.
Course name: Rensselaer Manufacturing and Prototyping Laboratories-Safety Orientation-6.0

According to the saftey quiz, if we are to cut MDF wood we must work in either the Architecture Fabrication Shop or the JEC Machine Shop.
